# Journey Spine

Status: Approved for v1 implementation planning.

## 1. Discover Chouinard Studios

Visitor arrives from search, social, a shared link, an audiobook platform, music platform, or direct navigation.

Goal: understand within seconds that Chouinard Studios is a premium, active creative studio producing music, audiobooks, and related work.

Desired next actions: explore current work, listen, browse stories, browse music, learn about the studio, or follow an external destination.

## 2. Discover an Audiobook

Visitor encounters a featured story, collection, search result, recommendation, or title page.

Goal: quickly understand what the work is, who it may appeal to, why this production is distinctive, and hear a preview where available.

Desired next actions: choose an external platform to listen or buy, explore related titles/collections, or return to the broader catalog.

## 3. Discover Music

Visitor sees latest music, a current project, shared track, or release.

Goal: listen immediately where practical, understand the project context, and continue to the appropriate external platform if desired.

Desired next actions: play/embed, visit SoundCloud/Spotify/Apple Music or another destination, explore more current studio work.

## 4. Understand the Craft

Visitor explores the studio, a production note, behind-the-scenes item, instrument/gear context, or recording process.

Goal: develop trust and appreciation for the care, musicianship, storytelling, production knowledge, and environment behind Chouinard Studios productions.

Desired next actions: return to the work itself, follow current activity, or inquire about a relevant collaboration/business matter.

## 5. Return and See What Is New

Returning visitor lands on the homepage, Music, Stories, or Current Work.

Goal: immediately detect meaningful new activity without the studio needing to manually redesign the site.

Desired next actions: play the newest item, explore a recent release/project, or revisit a changed curated section.

## 6. Explore What Inspires the Studio

Visitor encounters a curated item, playlist, artist, recording, gear note, or production/storytelling source.

Goal: discover something worthwhile while learning more about Chouinard Studios' taste and creative perspective.

Desired next actions: visit the original source, continue exploring Chouinard Studios content, or return later for new selections.

## 7. Make an Inquiry

Visitor has a legitimate collaboration, licensing, business, press, distribution, special-project, or selective production inquiry.

Goal: understand that inquiries are welcome without mistaking Chouinard Studios for a conventional hourly studio or narrator-for-hire business.

Desired next action: submit a concise inquiry through the approved contact path.

## Journey Guardrails

- Do not require accounts for v1 discovery journeys.
- Do not build direct audiobook commerce or entitlement flows.
- Do not force visitors off-site before they can understand or preview the work where an on-site preview/embed is practical.
- Preserve music as the flagship brand identity while optimizing audiobook discovery for near-term audience acquisition.
- Keep the experience flexible as the real catalog and release cadence emerge.
