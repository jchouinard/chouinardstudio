# Curated Freshness

## Status

Founder-approved direction, v1.

## Purpose

Chouinard Studios should feel alive and culturally connected without requiring the studio to create every piece of public-facing content itself.

The site may therefore combine original Chouinard Studios activity with a restrained layer of outside material that reflects the studio's taste, influences, listening habits, production interests, and creative world.

## Principle

Curate, do not scrape.

Outside material should be surfaced through official embeds, public feeds, source metadata, approved APIs, or links back to the original creator or publisher. Chouinard Studios should add a small amount of original perspective or context when appropriate rather than republishing complete articles, photographs, reviews, or other copyrighted work.

## Candidate Experiences

Possible sections include:

- On Our Radar
- From the Listening Room
- Studio Finds
- Currently Playing at Chouinard Studios
- Gear We Love
- Artists / recordings that influence the studio
- Production or storytelling material worth sharing

Names are working labels and may change during design.

## Homepage Hierarchy

Curated outside material is secondary to Chouinard Studios' own work.

The homepage should prioritize:

1. current Chouinard Studios work
2. audiobooks, music, and projects
3. studio craft and environment
4. selected outside material that inspires or interests the studio

The site must not read as a music-news blog, feed aggregator, or generic content farm.

## Automation Direction

The system may later ingest candidates from deliberately approved feeds, playlists, channels, manufacturer sources, or other public endpoints. Candidate material may be reviewed before being featured.

Where practical, activity on external platforms may flow into Chouinard Studios automatically. Examples include a maintained playlist, SoundCloud stream, or other approved source whose current contents can be reflected on the site without duplicate manual entry.

Automation must never fabricate activity, editorial endorsement, popularity, release dates, or relationships.

## Editorial Standard

Every curated item should answer at least one of these questions:

- Why does this matter to Chouinard Studios?
- What does this reveal about the studio's taste?
- What might a listener, musician, reader, or creator discover from it?
- How does it connect to music, storytelling, sound, craft, or the creative process?

Curated freshness exists to strengthen the Chouinard Studios identity, not merely to increase page volume.
