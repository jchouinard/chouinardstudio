# Chouinard Studios Brand Direction v1

## Public Brand

Public-facing brand name: Chouinard Studios

Technical identifiers:

- domain: chouinardstudio.com
- repository: chouinardstudio

The singular domain and repository name do not change the public brand name. Customer-facing experiences should consistently use Chouinard Studios.

## Brand Hierarchy

Music is the flagship creative expression of Chouinard Studios.

Audiobook publishing and storytelling are major Chouinard Studios businesses and may receive greater near-term commercial development effort, but they do not redefine the company as primarily an audiobook brand.

Chouinard Studios should feel like a serious creative production company whose audiobook imprint, music, and media all belong to one coherent world.

## Brand Personality

Chouinard Studios should feel:

- warm
- exceptional
- creative
- musical
- human
- timeless
- sophisticated
- craft-driven
- inviting
- cinematic

It should not feel:

- corporate
- sterile
- SaaS-like
- childish
- cheap
- generic
- trend-driven for its own sake
- influencer-driven
- aggressively commercial
- like a gear catalog

Premium, but never pretentious.

## Visual World

The site should feel as though it belongs inside the Chouinard Studios dream studio environment.

Primary visual ingredients:

- deep warm charcoal and near-black surfaces
- dark walnut hardwood and rich wood grain
- burgundy and oxblood accents
- restrained brass / antique-gold details
- warm ivory typography
- black leather
- Persian-style rugs and rich textiles
- intimate pools of warm practical light
- acoustic treatment integrated as architecture
- boutique instruments and amplifiers
- premium microphones and recording hardware
- modern production technology inside a timeless handcrafted environment

The visual experience should combine old-world craft with contemporary production capability.

## Reference Equipment / Atmosphere

The current visual reference set strongly features:

- burgundy Two-Rock amplifier and matching cabinet
- Friedman amplifier
- Orange cabinet
- premium guitar wall
- grand piano / Fazioli-inspired piano presence
- drum kit
- microphones positioned as working tools rather than props
- control-center / recording workstation
- acoustic clouds, wood diffusers, bass treatment, and warm architectural lighting
- dark walnut flooring
- Persian-style rugs
- black leather seating

These elements communicate taste, seriousness, musicianship, and sound obsession. The website should use that emotional language without becoming a gear showroom.

## Photography & Imagery

Photography should feel cinematic, dimensional, and editorial.

Prefer:

- low-key lighting
- warm highlights
- deep shadows with readable detail
- close-up craftsmanship shots
- wide studio environment images
- instruments in context
- recording moments
- real current work
- selective behind-the-scenes details

Avoid bright stock-studio imagery, generic musicians, sterile white rooms, neon cyber aesthetics, or images that feel disconnected from the actual Chouinard Studios environment.

## Typography & Layout Direction

The site should lean toward an elegant editorial feel rather than a software-product aesthetic.

Likely characteristics:

- refined serif or display typography for major brand moments
- highly legible supporting sans-serif text
- generous negative space
- strong visual hierarchy
- restrained use of gold/brass accents
- large immersive photography
- subtle texture
- minimal, deliberate animation

Exact fonts and design tokens are not yet approved.

## Experience Principle

The site should not open with a conventional services pitch such as "professional audio services."

The first impression should communicate:

This is a place where remarkable things are being made.

The work, environment, sound, and creative activity should carry as much of the brand message as the copy.

## Working Positioning Language

Possible future directions include:

- Music. Stories. Sound.
- Where music and stories come to life.

These are working explorations only and are not approved taglines.

## Professional Credibility Principle

Chouinard Studios should demonstrate quality through real work, process, environment, production knowledge, and attention to detail.

Do not rely on unsupported claims of professionalism. Show enough evidence that visitors naturally understand the level of craft involved.
