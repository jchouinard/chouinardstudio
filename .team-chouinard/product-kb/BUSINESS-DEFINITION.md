# Chouinard Studios Business Definition v1

## Business Identity

Chouinard Studios is an independent, family-led music, recording, publishing, and creative production studio.

It exists primarily to create, produce, publish, and develop its own work rather than operate as a conventional studio-for-hire.

Music is the creative heart and flagship expression of Chouinard Studios.

The business also develops premium audiobook and storytelling productions, original media, and other creative works that fit the Chouinard Studios standard of craftsmanship.

The long-term ambition is for "A Chouinard Studios Production" to communicate a recognizable standard of quality, taste, warmth, performance, and production.

## What We Produce

### Music & Original Works — Flagship

Music is the top-level creative identity of Chouinard Studios.

The studio produces its own original music and recordings. The music presence should feel active and current rather than limited to occasional finished releases.

Possible public expressions include:

- latest music
- works in progress
- finished recordings
- current projects
- SoundCloud integration
- studio performances
- original compositions
- production experiments
- future commercial releases

Chouinard Studios is not primarily marketed as a commercial recording facility. Outside music-production opportunities may be accepted selectively through inquiry, but launch does not include hourly studio rental, standardized production packages, or a rate card.

### Audiobooks & Storytelling — Primary Near-Term Commercial Business

Chouinard Studios develops and publishes its own curated audiobook and storytelling catalog.

The objective is not simply to produce individual audiobooks. The business is building an imprint, catalog, audience, and body of work that can compound over time.

The catalog should emphasize work that is warm, entertaining, timeless, uplifting, human, well-written, and enduring, with family appeal where appropriate.

Jen's narration and character work are part of the creative identity of Chouinard Studios productions. She is not positioned as a narrator for hire.

Andrew's recording, editing, production, mastering, music, and content-creation capabilities support these productions. If his production expertise develops into something he later wants to offer commercially, outside production work may become an additional business line. That is not a launch requirement.

### Media & Creative Projects — Expansion Layer

Chouinard Studios may create additional media that naturally grows from its music and storytelling capabilities, including original storytelling, educational media, podcasts, music for media, video, performance content, production-focused content, family-oriented creative work, and special collaborations.

This category should remain flexible. Do not manufacture services merely to make the company appear larger.

## Business Model

The underlying strategy is to create assets, not merely bill hours.

Where practical, Chouinard Studios should build things that continue producing value, including music masters, audiobook editions, publishing rights, catalogs, original intellectual property, audience relationships, recurring listeners, educational experiences, licensing opportunities, brand equity, and reusable production capability.

Custom production work may provide revenue later, but the core business should increasingly own the work, audience, or intellectual property it creates.

## Audience Priority

### Primary

Audiobook listeners, readers, and families looking for beautifully performed, warm, thoughtful, entertaining, and enduring stories.

### Secondary

Music listeners and followers of the studio's original work. Andrew's music should be allowed to grow organically alongside the audiobook business without forcing premature commercialization.

### Emerging

English-language learners and international audiences. Chouinard Studios may later use compelling English-language stories as a natural way to experience language and culture, potentially combining listening, text, vocabulary, cultural context, or guided learning.

### Additional

Artists, authors, publishers, producers, collaborators, educators, distributors, licensors, and others who discover Chouinard Studios and wish to explore a relationship. These are inquiry-driven rather than the site's primary launch acquisition target.

## Near-Term Operating Objective

Chouinard Studios is moving from studio investment and capability building into active production, publishing, audience growth, and commercialization.

Over the next twelve months, success should increasingly include:

- a growing audiobook catalog
- regular audiobook releases
- real distribution
- measurable audience growth
- recurring revenue
- original music being produced and shared
- a credible body of studio work
- increasing listener engagement
- direct audience relationships
- inbound opportunities
- a professional public presence
- an increasingly valuable catalog of owned creative assets

## Purpose of chouinardstudio.com

The website is the digital home of Chouinard Studios, not merely an online brochure.

Its jobs are to:

- establish the Chouinard Studios brand
- showcase original music and current work
- present and grow the audiobook catalog
- help people discover what to listen to
- communicate the studio's philosophy and quality
- demonstrate professional production capability through real work and process
- provide paths to listen, buy, follow, subscribe, or inquire
- build direct audience relationships
- surface new releases and ongoing projects
- provide an authoritative source for Chouinard Studios content
- create organic search opportunities
- eventually support educational and storytelling experiences
- provide real customer-intent and behavioral data for AI Support when that integration is ready

The website should feel alive. A returning visitor should reasonably expect to find new music, audiobook work, studio activity, or current projects.

## Living Studio Principle

Chouinard Studios should visibly demonstrate that creative work is happening.

Potential recurring content includes new tracks, audiobook releases and previews, recording sessions, works in progress, production notes, music clips, studio photographs, behind-the-scenes material, creative experiments, and selected production decisions.

This content is evidence of an active creative company, not content marketing for its own sake.

## Studio Craft & Credibility

The physical studio, equipment, workflow, and production knowledge are important parts of the brand and should be shown selectively.

The message is not "look how much expensive gear we own." The message is "this is the level of care we bring to what you hear."

The site may later include material about recording philosophy, microphone selection, guitar tone, piano recording, audiobook vocal production, room acoustics, editing, mastering, arrangement, performance, instruments, amplifiers, signal chain, and why specific production choices matter.

## Core Business Principle

Make work worth keeping.

This is an internal standard rather than an approved public tagline.
