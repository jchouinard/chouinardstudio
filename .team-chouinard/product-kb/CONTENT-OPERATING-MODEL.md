# Chouinard Studios Content Operating Model

## Status

Founder-approved direction, v1.

## Principle

Chouinard Studios should remain visibly current without requiring the Founder or Andrew to become website administrators.

ChatGPT Product Lead and Claude Engineering Lead will design, build, and maintain the website experience and supporting content system through the Team Chouinard bridge.

Andrew's primary role is to create the media and audio work that gives the site substance: music, recordings, audiobook production assets, release information, artwork, previews, and links to finished work on external platforms.

## Roles

### Founder

Provides business direction, approves major product and brand decisions, and supplies or confirms business information as needed.

### ChatGPT Product Lead

Owns content experience strategy, information architecture, presentation, prioritization, product quality, and ongoing product decisions.

### Claude Engineering Lead

Owns implementation, technical maintenance, content-system architecture, validation, deployment reliability, and safe updates to the site.

### Andrew / Creative Production

Creates and supplies the underlying creative media and release information, including as applicable:

- current and finished music
- SoundCloud links or embeds
- Apple Music links
- Spotify links
- audiobook release information
- audiobook distributor / retailer links
- audio previews or samples
- artwork and photography
- current project status
- production notes or behind-the-scenes material

Andrew should not need to understand the website codebase to keep the public site current.

## Content-System Requirement

The site architecture must make recurring content additions easy and low-risk.

Adding or updating an audiobook, music release, current project, external listening link, artwork item, or studio update should not require redesigning pages or changing application structure.

Content should be represented through a structured, reusable model that separates content data from visual presentation.

The initial implementation should prefer the lightest maintainable approach that supports the required publishing workflow. A heavy CMS is not required at launch unless the implementation demonstrates a clear operational need.

The content model should be designed so a CMS or other editorial interface can be added later without requiring a wholesale redesign of the website or catalog.

## Currentness Principle

The public experience should not depend on a rigid editorial calendar.

Instead, the site should support an ongoing stream of real studio activity. When meaningful new work exists, it should be easy to surface it in areas such as:

- Latest from the Studio
- Latest Music
- Current Projects
- Audiobook Releases
- Coming Soon
- Studio Notes / Behind the Work

The system should allow items to be published, updated, reordered, featured, archived, or regrouped without hard-coding the site's business direction.

## External Platform Principle

Chouinard Studios owns the branded discovery and presentation experience.

Finished works may live on appropriate third-party distribution or streaming platforms. The site should maintain clean external destinations for each work rather than trying to recreate those platforms.

Examples include SoundCloud, Apple Music, Spotify, audiobook retailers, audiobook streaming services, or future distribution partners.

The external-platform list must remain extensible rather than being hard-coded to today's providers.

## Future Automation

As the operating workflow becomes clear, ChatGPT and Claude may introduce automation that reduces manual site-maintenance work, for example validating external links, surfacing stale content, generating draft release entries from structured inputs, or making routine publication easier.

Automation should assist the creative workflow rather than force Andrew or the Founder into a complex content-management process.

Exact automation, CMS, repository content format, asset hosting, and editorial interfaces remain engineering/product decisions to be made during implementation planning.
