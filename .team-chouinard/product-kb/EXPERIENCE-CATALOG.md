# Experience Catalog

Status: Approved for v1 implementation planning.

## Home

A cinematic, living front door to Chouinard Studios. It should establish the brand immediately, show current creative activity, surface featured audiobook/story content, latest music, studio craft, current projects, and clear paths to listen, explore, follow, or inquire.

## Music

A home for original Chouinard Studios music and current musical work. Support current tracks, works in progress where appropriate, released works, external destinations such as SoundCloud / Spotify / Apple Music, and project context. Music is the flagship creative identity, but it should not be forced into premature commercialization.

## Stories

The audiobook and storytelling catalog. Support individual titles, collections, themed groupings, flexible release waves, previews, synopsis, audience guidance where useful, credits, artwork, and multiple external listen / buy destinations. Purchases happen on external distribution platforms.

## Studio

A brand and credibility experience centered on the production environment, craft, process, instruments, recording approach, and the care behind the work. It is not a rate-card services page or gear showroom.

## Current Work

A flexible stream of meaningful studio activity: latest music, audiobook work, recording sessions, production notes, current projects, coming-soon items, and selected behind-the-scenes material.

## Curated Freshness

A restrained layer of outside content that reflects Chouinard Studios' taste and influences: listening-room selections, artists, recordings, gear, production ideas, storytelling material, or other creative sources. Curate and link/embed; do not scrape or republish. This remains secondary to Chouinard Studios' own work.

## About

The people, purpose, creative philosophy, and story behind Chouinard Studios. Premium and human; not corporate biography language.

## Contact / Inquiries

A simple inquiry path for collaborations, licensing, business matters, special projects, and selective outside opportunities. Do not present standardized service packages, hourly studio rental, or narrator-for-hire offerings.

## Cross-cutting Experience Requirements

- The site should feel alive even when maintenance time is limited.
- One structured content item should be reusable across multiple relevant surfaces without duplicate entry.
- Visitors should be able to hear previews or embedded music without immediately leaving the site where licensing/platform support permits.
- Full purchases and primary third-party listening remain external.
- Exact public navigation labels may evolve during design without changing these approved experience responsibilities.
