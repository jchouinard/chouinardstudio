# Founder Decisions

This file records approved Chouinard Studios product decisions made by the Founder.

## Current Decisions

- Chouinard Studios is an independent Team Chouinard product and operating business.
- Public brand name: Chouinard Studios.
- Repository name: chouinardstudio.
- Domain: chouinardstudio.com.
- The singular domain and repository name do not change the public-facing plural brand name.
- Chouinard Studios remains separate from AI Support, Decision Widget, and Piksake.
- Chouinard Studios will later become a real AI Support customer and Customer Zero when AI Support is ready.
- Music is the flagship creative expression of the Chouinard Studios brand.
- Chouinard Studios primarily creates and produces its own music and creative work rather than market itself as a conventional studio-for-hire.
- Outside music or production opportunities may be considered selectively through inquiry, but hourly rental, rate-card services, and standardized production packages are not launch offerings.
- Chouinard Studios develops and publishes its own curated audiobook and storytelling catalog as a real publishing imprint and long-term owned asset.
- Jen's narration is part of Chouinard Studios productions and is not marketed as narrator-for-hire work.
- Andrew's recording, editing, mastering, music, and content-production capabilities are developed first for Chouinard Studios' own work. External production services may be considered later only if he chooses to pursue them.
- The primary near-term audience-acquisition focus is audiobook listeners, readers, and families.
- Andrew's music should be allowed to grow organically alongside the audiobook business without forcing premature commercialization.
- Chouinard Studios should visibly reflect ongoing creative production through current work, latest music, audiobook activity, and selected behind-the-scenes content.
- The website should establish professional studio credibility through real work, process, environment, and attention to detail rather than unsupported claims or gear-showroom presentation.
- The visual direction is premium, warm, cinematic, craft-driven, musical, timeless, and human, using the dream-studio reference environment as the primary aesthetic inspiration.
- The website is the digital home of the business, not merely a brochure. It should support discovery, listening, catalog growth, audience relationships, inquiries, and future AI Support learning.
- Product definition must be sufficiently approved before full website implementation begins.
